03

How I Work

A process built
around people.

01

Discover

Research is the foundation. I begin by understanding the problem space — conducting user interviews, competitive analysis, and stakeholder conversations to uncover real needs rather than assumed ones.

02

Define

Synthesising research into clear insights. I map user journeys, build personas, and frame design problems in terms of user goals — turning data into a focused design direction.

03

Ideate

Exploring the solution space widely before narrowing. Sketching, workshops, and rapid ideation to generate diverse approaches, then evaluating them against user needs and business constraints.

04

Design

Translating ideas into experiences — wireframes, information architecture, interaction flows, and high-fidelity prototypes. Every decision is grounded in the research phase.

05

Test & Refine

Validating with real users. Usability testing, feedback synthesis, and iteration cycles to ensure the solution genuinely works before it ships — and after.
