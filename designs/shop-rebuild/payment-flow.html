Choose how this payment works. You’ll enter the cost in the next step.

1. What kind of payment is this? *

Choose an item type. Fixed Price is the default.

2. How can people access it?

An open payment link works with any item type, including Customer chooses amount.

3. How can people pay?

Set the payment count here. Enter the amount in the next step.

4. When should this payment become inactive? Inactive payments are hidden from parents and from the dashboard’s Active payments view. Schools can still find them by switching the filter to Inactive payments.

On this date, the payment is removed from the parent app and becomes Inactive in the dashboard. It leaves the Active payments view. To find it again, change the dashboard filter to Inactive payments.
