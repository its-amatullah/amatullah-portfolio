P-Town

A lively board game where players journey through Pune's iconic areas in an auto-rickshaw — facing challenges on the way to the finish line.

Year 2024
Role Game Designer & Visual Designer

Overview Scope Identity & Cards Board & Poster

Overview

Experience, explore, strategise

P-Town is a board game inspired by Pune, where players travel between recognisable areas of the city in an auto-rickshaw. Players encounter challenges and obstacles, choosing paths and reaching area checkpoints on their way to the finish line. Movement is determined by a spinning wheel.

The project involved designing the complete game identity — from logo through to board, cards, and poster — as a single cohesive visual system.

Identity & Cards

Logo & game card system

The P-Town logo draws on Pune's cultural iconography — a bold wordmark paired with city-inspired illustration. The game card system covers the four card types used during play, each with a distinct visual treatment to aid quick recognition during the game.

Board & Poster

The game in full

The board layout uses a map-inspired composition of Pune, with the auto-rickshaw route winding through recognisable area checkpoints. The poster presents the complete P-Town game with all components visible, designed for the college design exhibition.
