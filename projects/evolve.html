Overview

Fitness, on your terms

The Evolve Gym Interactive Display Screen lets members monitor workouts, access personalised plans, review schedules, and track BMI with ease. Featuring a sleek, dark-themed design, the system is available on both mobile and kiosk, fostering community and interaction for enhanced fitness experiences.

The Evolve brand is built on a bold black and yellow palette — high contrast, gym-appropriate, and immediately recognisable on display screens in a gym environment.

Kiosk Screens

Landing, sign-up & home

The kiosk experience starts with an Evolve-branded landing screen, moves through a sign-up flow, and opens into a personalised home screen with quick access to workout goals, plans, exercise demos, and class schedules.

Interactive display — landing, sign-up, home and daily schedule

Extended Features

Class schedule, demos & community

The extended feature set covers the full member journey: a weekly class schedule with class details and difficulty levels, a video-based exercise demonstration library, BMI report history, and a connections screen showing gym buddies, achievements, and posts.
